RadiusSquare root of the leaves a segment feeds. Area-preserving, self-similar because subtree sizes are Zipfian.
LengthOne named node, one segment: the measured falling length ratio is inherent. Chains at the twigs, fans at the limbs.
AngleGolden-angle phyllotaxis above fan-out 2, planar fork at 2, straight run at 1, lean toward up.
CurrentBranches bend in a slow water current, more the higher they reach. A smooth noise over each branch’s resting position offsets its phase, amplitude and direction, so neighbours agree and distant branches differ. Dials on the right. Add ?grow to the URL to replay growth.
TintEach tree takes its colour from its rank in first-commit order along one ramp: violet for the oldest files, gold for the newest. Twigs darker than the trunk.
Leaves and lightLeaves glow brighter the more recently the file was edited. The ground ring is import fan-in: how many files reach for this one.
