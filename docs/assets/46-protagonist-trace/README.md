# 46 — The protagonist trace (rung 5 of #183)

**The punchline first.** One real note — Welch Labs' *The moment we stopped
understanding AI [AlexNet]*, captured 2026-08-14 — can now be read at every
level the system holds: as 1024 numbers drawn as a 32x32 image, as 32 lit
pixels in a 2,048-latent dictionary (60% of whose mass has a name), as a
resident of an atlas cell independently labeled *mechanistic
interpretability*, as a family of five neighbors two different lenses mostly
agree on, as a timeline whose segments speak the material while the document
speaks the lecture (section 45), and as one end of a great-circle journey
where its identity hands over to EpicMap's at t~0.5. The record has shown
the forest since section 01; this is one tree, growing.

**Why this note.** Chosen by measurement, not taste (section 42): its
loudest latent, #1597 "educational breakdown of language model mechanics",
was read off the dictionary at rung 0. The T2 discipline — one example,
never switched — has now held for five sections, and every panel here wears
the same CYAN thread.

and the shared three (Stephen Blum's mini-ChatGPT livecode, "Let's build

with.

**Figure 03 — the journey.** The note slerped along the great circle into

midpoint. Interpolation in this space is a feature handover, not a fade —
section 34's road finding, now watchable on one pair of real notes.

  the terrain (section 44, figure 03) point at the same cell, but neither
