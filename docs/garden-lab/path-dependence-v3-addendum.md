# Path-dependence v3 addendum: one engine, persistence staleness, terminal-only attach

2026-07-13. This run exists to answer `external-review-response-codex-v6.md`
(findings 1, 3, 9, and the cm question). Data: 192 cells in
`replay-cells-v3/*.json` (total grid wall time 1559.9 s), schema_version 3,
generated by `scripts/grove_lab/replay.py` at engine commit `e0e903c`
("replay engine v3 - one engine, parameterized semantics, new arms").
Grid: 3 buckets (epicmap n=2065, ai-building n=427, visual-craft n=86)
x 8 order/base arms (date at base 0.25/0.5/0.75; seeds 101-105 at base 0.5)
x 8 policies (rebuild-never, rebuild-0.1, rebuild-0.25, rebuild-0.25f15,
rebuild-1.0, cm-never, terminal-never, terminal-cm-never) = 24 comparable
arms per policy.

**SUPERSEDES v2 CELLS.** The v2 grid (`replay-cells/*.json`, 144 cells plus
the 24 post-K2 cm reruns and 24 hybrid cells) mixed engine versions: the K2
centroid fix changed a shared helper and only cm + hybrid cells were rerun
(v6 finding 1). Any cross-policy comparison must now cite
`replay-cells-v3/*.json` only. v2 cells remain on disk for provenance.

**Engine version audit.** 191/192 cells stamp `engine_commit: e0e903c`. One
cell, `replay-cells-v3/visual-craft-105-0.5-terminal-cm-never.json`, stamps

`docs/grove-lab/shader-brainstorm-handoff.md`); the engine code is

Noted rather than hidden.

**Centroid semantics (v6 finding 1 fix).** `centroid_mode` is stamped per
cell: `production` for rebuild-never, all theta arms, and terminal-never
(production-faithful, what dendro.py ships); `descendant` only for the two
cm arms, whose maintained centroids require the descendant initialization

descendant-semantics engine anymore — the cm arms differ in POLICY, not in
engine accident.

Consistency check: the production-faithful arms reproduce the v2 report
values exactly at date-0.5 (epicmap never final ARI 0.085 / mass L1 0.685;

## 2. Persistence staleness — the unmeasured visual channel (v6 finding 3)

New per-checkpoint metric `production.persistence` in every cell:
persistence (branch LENGTH, the renderer's limb-length input) is
max-normalized per tree, nodes matched across trees by descendant-set
Jaccard >= 0.3, then `l1_mean` = mean |difference| over matched pairs and
`spearman` = rank correlation (`replay.py::persistence_metric`). Attach
never updates persistence; neither does centroid maintenance.

Final-checkpoint values, mean over the 8 arms per bucket (date-0.5 arm in
parentheses):

| bucket | policy | pers L1 | pers Spearman |
|---|---|---|---|
| epicmap | never | 0.225 (0.129) | 0.670 (0.536) |
| epicmap | theta=0.25 | 0.112 (0.173) | 0.805 (0.764) |
| epicmap | theta=0.1 | 0.107 (0.173) | 0.811 (0.764) |
| ai-building | never | 0.315 (0.102) | 0.023 (0.100) |
| ai-building | theta=0.25 | 0.018 (0.004) | 0.774 (0.986) |
| ai-building | theta=0.1 | 0.009 (0.001) | 0.904 (0.991) |
| visual-craft | never | 0.216 (0.250) | -0.200 (-0.400) |
| visual-craft | theta=0.25 | 0.026 (0.001) | 0.936 (1.000) |
| visual-craft | theta=0.1 | 0.049 (0.010) | 0.893 (1.000) |

Checkpoint-mean L1 over all checkpoints and arms per bucket: epicmap never
0.128 vs theta=0.25 0.088 vs theta=0.1 0.066; ai-building 0.289 / 0.131 /
0.109; visual-craft 0.142 / 0.101 / 0.075.

**Branch length is substantially stale under never-rebuild, and its rank
order collapses on two of three buckets.** By final checkpoints the mean
matched-node discrepancy is 0.22-0.32 of tree-max persistence, and the
Spearman falls to ~0 in ai-building (0.023) and goes negative in
visual-craft (-0.200) — the never-rebuild tree's LONGEST limbs are not the
fresh tree's longest limbs; ordinal limb-length reading is unreliable, not
merely noisy. Theta arms hold L1 at 0.01-0.11 with Spearman 0.77-0.94.
Centroid maintenance does not touch this channel (cm-never: 0.218 / 0.320
/ 0.216 — within noise of plain never, as expected since cm never rewrites
persistence), and neither does terminal-only attach (final-checkpoint
pers L1 ties rebuild-never in 20/24 arms). v6 finding 3's worry is
confirmed: this was a real, renderer-visible drift channel that only
rebuilds repair.

Caveat: matched-pair counts are small (3-15 per checkpoint, median 6;
266 of 912 checkpoint observations match fewer than 5 pairs), so
per-checkpoint Spearman values are coarse — read the bucket-level pattern,
not any single rho.

## 3. Terminal-only attach (v6 finding 9)

New cell field `attach_internal_targets` counts attaches whose chosen
target was an internal node or the root — placements a fresh fit would
never produce. Under production attach this is common, not rare: summed
over the 8 never-rebuild arms per bucket, epicmap 2429/8263 attaches
(29.4%), ai-building 471/1705 (27.6%), visual-craft 130/344 (37.8%)
(`*-rebuild-never.json`). The theta arms span 20.7-37.8%, with bucket-level
shares (epicmap / ai-building / visual-craft): theta=0.1 32.5/20.7/31.1%,
theta=0.25 30.4/21.9/34.3%, 0.25+f15 30.4/21.9/33.1%, theta=1.0
29.6/25.1/37.8%. The terminal arms verify 0/8263, 0/1705, 0/344 by
construction.

Head-to-head, terminal-never vs rebuild-never over 24 arms: terminal wins
23/24 on final ARI, 23/24 on final and AUC triplet, 23/24 on ARI-AUC, and
19/24 on mass L1 (the sole ARI/triplet loss is
`visual-craft-date-0.75-terminal-never.json`, 0.369 vs 0.465). Grid means:

| policy | final ARI | mass L1 | ARI-AUC |
|---|---|---|---|
| rebuild-never (production attach) | 0.368 | 0.411 | 0.495 |
| terminal-never | 0.484 | 0.269 | 0.623 |
| terminal-cm-never | 0.437 | 0.323 | 0.593 |
| theta=0.25 (production attach) | 0.801 | 0.079 | 0.740 |

**Terminal-only attach materially narrows the drift but does not close
it.** Against the never-to-theta=0.25 gap it recovers roughly 27% of final
ARI ((0.484-0.368)/(0.801-0.368)), 43% of mass L1, and 52% of ARI-AUC —
a real, near-free improvement from a strictly local change to the attach
rule, and confirmation that internal-node filing is a genuine drift
mechanism (v6 finding 9 vindicated). It is NOT a substitute for rebuilds:
terminal-never's remaining drift (final ARI 0.484, mass L1 0.269) is still
far from any theta arm, and it repairs neither capacity starvation nor
persistence staleness (section 2). Adding maintained centroids on top
makes it slightly worse, not better (terminal-cm loses to plain terminal
on mass L1 in 15/24 arms, ARI-AUC 14/24).

Untested combination: terminal-only attach BETWEEN theta rebuilds (the
grid crosses attach rule with never/cm only). Given terminal's 23/24
between-rebuild advantage, it plausibly shallows the theta arms'
transients; if terminal attach ships with the rebuild policy, one
confirmation arm should be run.

## 4. Corrected centroid-maintain under one engine

cm-never (descendant-initialized maintained centroids, production attach)
vs rebuild-never over 24 arms: cm wins 6 / loses 14 on final ARI, wins 5 /
loses 18 on ARI-AUC, and is a coin flip on hierarchy and mass (final
triplet 12W/11L, triplet-AUC 12W/10L, mass L1 11W/11L). Date-0.5 spot
checks: epicmap ARI 0.074 vs never 0.085, ai-building 0.635 vs 0.625,
visual-craft 0.150 vs 0.317 (`*-date-0.5-cm-never.json`).

**Verdict: wash on hierarchy and mass, mildly worse on assignment — the v2
"wash against doing nothing" conclusion survives the engine fix and firms
up on the assignment side.** Online centroid drift moves attach targets
around without ever correcting structure, persistence, or capacity, and it
costs running-sum state. Not recommended in any configuration measured
(alone, or under terminal attach).

## 5. Limitations

- **The date arm is still not ingest order.** Dates remain upload/content
  dates with ties randomized and undated notes interleaved at seeded
  positions (epicmap 2044/21 dated/undated, ai-building 385/42,
  visual-craft 80/6, stamped per cell); n=1 temporal path per bucket per
  base. `ingested_at` capture has since landed in store.py (commit
  `922ff01`), but its history only accumulates from now on — no
  retroactive production-order replay is possible.
- **AUC is still an unweighted checkpoint mean, not a time-weighted
  integral.** Nulls are dropped (15 of 912 checkpoint observations have
  unscoreable triplet agreement); rankings leaning on AUC inherit
  checkpoint-phase sensitivity, and theta=1.0's final-checkpoint wins
  remain artifacts of trigger position.
- **Persistence matching is thin**: Jaccard >= 0.3 descendant matching
  yields 3-15 pairs per checkpoint (median 6); unmatched nodes contribute
  nothing, so pers L1, like mass L1, is a matched lower bound.
- **epicmap flat ARI is still reference-noise-bound** (intrinsic
  cross-half ARI ~0.14); epicmap policy weight stays on triplet AUC and
  mass L1.
- **visual-craft is small** (86 notes): single-note reassignments move ARI
  a lot, and it is the only bucket where the f15 floor binds — its
  0.25-vs-f15 deltas are the designed behavior of the floor on a tiny
  tree, measured, not projected.
- **One cell stamps the docs-only successor commit** (`7b30120`,
  `visual-craft-105-0.5-terminal-cm-never.json`); engine code identical to
  `e0e903c` (see audit above).
- **Terminal attach x theta rebuild is unmeasured** (section 3).
- `fit_nodes_triplet` still has no structural null of its own (v6 finding
  4); that gap gates decision (b), not this addendum.
